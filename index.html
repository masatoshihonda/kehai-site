気配  ·  ke-hai  / keh-high /

A sense of presence: the feeling that someone or something is there, or that something is about to change, before you can see it or put it into words.

That is the idea behind our name. KEHAI builds AI infrastructure that senses the energy around it, from power and grid conditions to heat, and adapts where and how AI runs.
